Our Story

Boss Hoss Cycles, Inc. ignited the V-8 motorcycle scene in 1990. Monte Warne, a visionary pilot and aviation technician, engineered the first V-8 motorcycle, creating a unique machine with a classic cruiser aesthetic.

Monte's innovative single-speed transmission design was key. This ingenious right-angle drive allows for a compact bike profile, keeping the engine aligned with the frame for unparalleled balance.

This groundbreaking design ensures a riding experience unlike any other V-8 motorcycle on the market, offering raw power with exceptional handling.

FAQs

What powers these machines?

Each Boss Hoss motorcycle is powered by a potent Chevy V-8 engine, offering unparalleled performance and a thrilling ride.

Who started Boss Hoss?

The company was founded by Monte Warne, a visionary with a passion for aviation and engineering.

What makes the design unique?

The innovative single-speed transmission and inline engine mounting create a balanced and distinctive motorcycle.

Where did it all begin?

Boss Hoss Cycles began in a 5000 square foot shop in Dyersburg, TN, where the first V-8 motorcycle was created.

What is the core philosophy?

Boss Hoss is about creating powerful, stylish, and unique motorcycles that provide an unmatched riding experience.
